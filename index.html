ABOUT THE RESEARCHER

Curiosity guided by evidence.

I am building an independent research practice around real-world problems and questions that deserve careful investigation. My approach is to study existing evidence, define questions clearly, document sources, identify uncertainty, and develop practical next steps.

I welcome constructive feedback and collaboration with researchers, educators, technical specialists, community organizations, and institutions. I aim to communicate honestly about what is known, what remains uncertain, and what needs testing.

AREA 01

Environmental technology & circular resources

AREA 02

Water & wastewater management

Reviewing affordable treatment and reuse approaches with attention to water quality, public health, environmental impact, and the need for appropriate testing.

  • Evidence review and technology comparison
  • Safe water reuse questions
  • Low-cost, context-appropriate solutions

AREA 03

Historical medical texts & modern evidence

Developing a structured way to connect claims in Ibn Sina’s Canon of Medicine with modern identities, published research, and safety information.

  • Source and translation tracking
  • Botanical identification as a research question
  • Evidence and safety review—not treatment advice

HOW I WORK

Trust is built through transparent research.

Good research is a process. The methods, limits, and evidence matter as much as the ambition.

01

Source carefully
Prefer credible primary sources, scholarly publications, and recognized standards.

02

State uncertainty
Separate verified facts, hypotheses, estimates, and unanswered questions.

03

Protect people and nature
Follow appropriate safety, ethical, and environmental requirements.

04

Report honestly
Document methods, limitations, setbacks, and results without exaggeration.
