Method and standards

Make the process as clear as the idea

Credible research needs more than a promising idea. Each project should have a focused question, traceable sources, a documented method, and honest limitations.

1

Define the question

State the problem, scope, and what would count as useful evidence.

2

Review existing evidence

Record reliable sources, dates, methods, and uncertainty.

3

Collect and check data

Use reproducible calculations and suitable safety or quality checks.

4

Publish limitations and next steps

Share only supportable conclusions and explain what remains untested.
